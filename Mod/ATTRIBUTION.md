## RimWorld and other integrations

RimWorld is by Ludeon Studios. Its vanilla recreation definitions were studied as
behavioral models; the patch refers to game-provided job drivers and joy giver classes.
No game assembly is distributed.

Vanilla Expanded Framework and Harmony are dependencies declared by Malay Themed
Expansion. They are not bundled here. Joy Rescue is an optional complementary tool;
it is not required and is not bundled.
